NemVai — Refund Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to all paid subscriptions purchased from NemVai, our real-time server status, HTTP/TCP monitoring, and incident tracking platform. All payments are processed securely by Paddle, our authorized payment provider.

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with a paid NemVai subscription, you may request a full refund within 14 days of the original purchase date. Refund requests received after the 14-day window will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at support@nemvai.app with the subject line "Refund Request" and include the email address used for the purchase and, if available, your Paddle transaction ID. We aim to process all valid requests within 5–10 business days.

3. How Refunds Are Issued

  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the purchase.
  • Refunds are processed by Paddle, and the time for the funds to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.
  • Partial refunds may be issued if the subscription was used for part of the billing period.
  • Once a refund is issued, access to the corresponding paid plan will be revoked.

4. Cancellations

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account dashboard. Cancellation stops future recurring charges, but access continues until the end of the current billing period. Amounts already charged for the current period are non-refundable, except as provided in Section 1.

5. Non-Refundable Items

  • The free plan is not subject to any refunds.
  • Fees charged by third parties (e.g., banks, card issuers, or currency conversion fees) are not refundable by NemVai.
  • Charges resulting from failure to cancel a subscription before renewal, unless requested within the 14-day window.

6. Contact Us

Questions about this Refund Policy? Contact us at support@nemvai.app, and we will be happy to help.
