NemVai — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NemVai ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our real-time server status, HTTP/TCP monitoring, and incident tracking platform (the "Service"). By using the Service, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, username, and password (stored securely using hashing).
  • Monitoring data: server names, URLs, and endpoints you add to be monitored, along with uptime, latency, and incident history generated by the Service.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, and diagnostics to help us improve the Service.
  • Payment data: we do not store your card details. Payments are processed by Paddle, our payment provider, under their own privacy policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Service and its features.
  • To authenticate your account and manage your subscription and plan entitlements.
  • To send service notifications, alerts, and important account or billing updates.
  • To provide support and respond to your requests.
  • To detect, prevent, and address security, fraud, and technical issues.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the Service and process payments, including our hosting provider (Render) and payment processor (Paddle), each contractually obligated to protect your data and use it solely to provide their services.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. Monitoring history, logs, and incident records are retained in accordance with the limits of your plan and may be permanently deleted after account deletion. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

5. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit (TLS), password hashing, and restricted access to production systems. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@nemvai.app. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local browser storage (e.g., for your authentication token and theme preference) and standard web technologies to improve your experience. You can clear this data from your browser at any time; some functionality may then require you to log in again.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on this page and, where appropriate, by email. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at support@nemvai.app.
